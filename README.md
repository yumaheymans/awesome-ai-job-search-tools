# Awesome AI Job Search Tools

A curated list of tools, extensions, and resources that help job seekers write stronger resumes, pass ATS screening, prepare for interviews, and manage the application process. Every entry is included because it does something genuinely useful, not because it paid to be here.

Maintained by the team at [VeloApply](https://veloapply.com). Contributions are welcome, see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Resume Builders

- [Rezi](https://www.rezi.ai) - AI resume builder with a real time ATS score that updates as you edit.
- [Teal](https://www.tealhq.com) - Resume builder combined with a job tracker and browser extension.
- [Kickresume](https://www.kickresume.com) - AI resume and cover letter builder with a large template library.
- [Novoresume](https://novoresume.com) - Structured resume builder with section by section AI writing help.
- [Zety](https://zety.com) - Template focused resume builder with a wide range of designs.
- [Reactive Resume](https://rxresu.me) - Free, open source resume builder you can self host.

## ATS Optimization and Resume Scanners

- [Jobscan](https://www.jobscan.co) - Scores a resume against a job description and flags keyword gaps.
- [Resume Optimizer Pro](https://resumeoptimizerpro.com) - Chrome extension that checks ATS parsing and keyword coverage.
- [VeloApply](https://veloapply.com) - ATS compatibility scoring and resume optimization built into the same workspace as its autofill extension.

## Job Application Autofill and Tracking

- [Simplify](https://simplify.jobs) - Free Chrome extension for autofilling job applications and tracking submissions.
- [Huntr](https://huntr.co) - Kanban style job tracker with unlimited free autofill.
- [Careerflow](https://www.careerflow.ai) - Job tracker with a free LinkedIn profile optimizer built in.
- [VeloApply](https://veloapply.com) - Chrome extension that autofills applications across ATS platforms including Workday and Greenhouse, with queue based batch apply, EEO field handling, and custom dropdown support. Built for user reviewed submissions, not auto submit.
- [Jobscan](https://www.jobscan.co) - Also offers an extension for on page match scoring while you apply.

## Interview Preparation

- [Google Interview Warmup](https://grow.google/certificates/interview-warmup) - Free tool from Google for quick interview practice with instant feedback.
- [Pramp](https://www.pramp.com) - Free peer to peer mock interview practice, now part of Exponent.
- [Yoodli](https://www.yoodli.ai) - AI feedback on speaking delivery, pacing, and communication clarity.
- [Big Interview](https://biginterview.com) - AI graded mock interview practice across a wide range of roles.
- [Interviewing.io](https://interviewing.io) - Live, anonymous mock interviews with real engineers for technical roles.

## LinkedIn Optimization

- [Careerflow](https://www.careerflow.ai) - Free LinkedIn profile optimizer bundled with a job tracker.
- [Jobscan](https://www.jobscan.co) - LinkedIn profile scoring alongside resume and keyword analysis.
- [Kickresume](https://www.kickresume.com) - LinkedIn profile review as part of its resume toolkit.

## Job Search Platforms and Aggregators

- [CareJobs.Now](https://carejobs.now/) - Free job board for US healthcare workers, with 170,000+ openings and the pay each employer posts.
- [LinkedIn Jobs](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs) - The largest professional network's job board.
- [Indeed](https://www.indeed.com) - One of the largest general job search aggregators.
- [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com) - Job listings paired with company reviews and salary reports.
- [Adzuna](https://www.adzuna.com) - Job search aggregator with a public API used by developers to build search tools.

## Salary and Compensation Research

- [Levels.fyi](https://www.levels.fyi) - Crowdsourced compensation data, strongest for tech roles.
- [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com) - Company reviews paired with self reported salary data.
- [Payscale](https://www.payscale.com) - Salary benchmarking by role, location, and experience.

## Open Source Projects

- [Reactive Resume](https://github.com/AmruthPillai/Reactive-Resume) - MIT licensed, self hostable resume builder.
- [JSON Resume](https://jsonresume.org) - Open standard for representing a resume as structured JSON, with a themeable renderer.
